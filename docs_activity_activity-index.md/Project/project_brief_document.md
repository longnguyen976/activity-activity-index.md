## Problem

Visitors at the park often experience long waiting lines for physical tickets, lack interactive navigation around historical lotus sites, and miss out on cultural storytelling. Furthermore, park management lacks real-time data on visitor flow, commercial space rental performance, and effective tools for targeted marketing campaigns.

## Goal of the project

Provide a seamless mobile-first platform where visitors can purchase E-tickets, navigate via an interactive 2.5D map, engage in gamified checkpoint challenges with rewards, and share photos in a social gallery. Enable commercial partners to request space rentals and empower park administrators to manage campaigns, analyze visitor insights, and monitor system infrastructure. Agreed measures are in the CVCA and in money terms in [W6 cash flow](w6-cash-flow.md).
